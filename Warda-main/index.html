Sync BILLZ to Stock Book

Use one button for the complete routine sync: first the full product catalog (all colors, sizes, variants and available photos), then imports, transfers, write-offs and sales for the selected date range. Existing records are deduplicated where identifiers allow.

Automatic sync covers transaction history only. Run the full sync button after product, color, size or photo changes in BILLZ.

Advanced BILLZ tools

These are separate diagnostic or maintenance actions. You usually do not need them for a normal daily sync.

Repair sales: compare with BILLZ and remove duplicates

Compares BILLZ-synced sales in Stock Book with BILLZ for every store and date, including gross sales and returns separately. Manual sales are not archived. Where a day differs, only the BILLZ-synced rows are archived (they stay in Deleted and in a backup file that downloads first) and BILLZ sales are loaded again.

Careful: start fresh from BILLZ

Clean rebuild for a new BILLZ account: first refresh the complete BILLZ catalog, then archive active stock movements (they remain in Deleted), load BILLZ stock as the opening balance, and re-sync transactions from your chosen date. A full JSON backup including product photos is downloaded first. Existing catalog settings and non-stock records are kept. Review the preview before continuing.
