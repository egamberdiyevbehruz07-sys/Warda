TELEGRAM — DASHBOARD ONLY

All reports and product photos are shown inside the Mini App. No group or private-chat messages, scheduled reports, retries, manual reports, photo albums or product photos are sent.

Use Reports > Access Management to connect one private Telegram group. The bot must be an administrator. Group members are allowed to open the Dashboard. Add/remove members in Telegram itself.

Set TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN, SUPABASE_URL and SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY on the hosting service. Enable the Dashboard menu button once after deployment. /start is silent.
